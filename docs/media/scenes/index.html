Pulse · three new terminal scenes

Actual Braille-renderer output at 24 fps, driven by the same generated stereo spectrum score. These are implementation previews, not AI concept images or recordings of songs. Palette is Warm for comparison with the reference; Auto and all existing palettes also work.

Press s for the next scene or Shift+s for the previous one: Terrain → Endless Ribbons → Orbital Fragments → Filament Current. v changes looks, p colors, c cameras, and [/] detail. Scene switching preserves your settings.

Broad sheets and narrow strands taper, weave over and under each other, and carry travelling bends, twists, ribs and edge marks.
